Desk Companion

Your desk's own superhero.

A free desktop app where animated heroes remind you to drink water, stretch, eat lunch and go home on time, with a fresh quote to start every day.

What it does

💧

Water, stretching, lunch and going home, on your schedule. Your hero even hands you the water bottle.

A new one each morning, all year round, and a fun goodbye when it's time to head home.

🖼️

Your own pictures

Installing

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That's normal for small independent apps. Click More info, then Run anyway. Desk Companion appears in the system tray, next to the clock (check the ^ arrow). It updates itself from then on.

Mac says it can't open the app
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Desk Companion into Applications.
  2. Open it once. When macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. If macOS says the app "is damaged", run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Desk Companion.app"

Desk Companion lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

Which Mac download do I need?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, use the Apple Silicon version; if it says Intel, use the Intel version.
